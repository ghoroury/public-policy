Fits, Just Right

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Applies to Fits, Just Right on iPhone and Android · Developer: Ghoroury

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are stored on your device. We don't run servers of our own.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob, and app measurement from Google Analytics for Firebase. Both follow the consent choices you make in the game, and on iPhone, ad tracking happens only if you allow it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so you can keep playing:

  • your level progress and results;
  • your coin and undo-token balances, and a record of how you earned and spent them;
  • your season pass progress and the rewards you've claimed;
  • the decorations and cosmetics you own;
  • your settings, such as sound and reduced motion.

The game also keeps a simple record of in-game events on your device, for example "level completed" or "hint used", to help balance the game. That record stays on your device.

This information stays on your device until you delete the game. Your device's own backup may also include it: an iCloud or computer backup on iPhone, or Google's Auto Backup on Android. Those backups are controlled by your device settings and by Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

2. Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases: Remove Forced Ads, and a monthly premium season pass.

  • Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), under their terms and privacy policies.
  • The game receives only a confirmation that a purchase is valid, so it can unlock it and restore it after a reinstall.
  • We don't receive your name, payment card or billing address.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, prevent fraud and measure how ads perform, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID on Android, and on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address and the approximate location it implies;
  • information about your device and the game, such as the model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, for example whether you saw or tapped one;
  • crash and performance data related to ads.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. App measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how many people install and play the game, and to measure which of our own ads bring new players. It records:

  • that the game was opened for the first time on a device;
  • a few game events: finishing the tutorial, clearing a level (the level number, and whether it was the first clear), and completing a purchase;
  • an app instance ID that Firebase creates for your installation, plus basic device information (model, operating system version, language, app version) and the approximate location implied by your IP address.

It does not record your name, email, contacts or precise location.

  • Consent first: measurement starts only after the game has your consent choices. Advertising identifiers and ad-related measurement are used only if your choices allow it, and on iPhone only if you allow tracking.
  • Who receives it: Google processes this data for us. Where your choices allow it, it's linked with Google Ads so we can see which of our ads led to an install.
  • How long it's kept: event data is kept for the shortest retention period Google Analytics offers, currently 2 months.

How Google handles Firebase data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): before showing ads or measuring, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form. If you don't consent, the game shows non-personalized ads only, and ad-related measurement stays off. The game plays the same either way. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings › Privacy › Privacy choices, where the consent form applies.
  • iPhone tracking: after the tutorial, iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA isn't shared. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, under Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some devices.
  • Remove Forced Ads: this purchase turns off the ads shown between levels. Optional reward videos, for hints and undos, play only if you choose to watch them.
  • Deleting your data: deleting the game deletes the game's data on your device. For Google's advertising data, use myadcenter.google.com. For measurement data, contact us and we'll ask Google to delete the data for your installation.

6. Children

The game is made for a general audience. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA, you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. We don't hold personal information about you on systems of our own. For any question or request, contact us at the address below. We don't sell personal information. Where a law calls ad personalization "sharing", you can opt out through the consent and tracking choices above.

8. Security

Your game data is stored in your device's standard app storage. We don't transmit it to servers of our own. Data handled by Apple and Google is protected under their security practices.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game's privacy practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Where a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the game's update notes.
