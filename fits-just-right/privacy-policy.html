Fits, Just Right

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 January 2027 · Applies to Fits, Just Right on iPhone and Android · Developer: Ghoroury

Fits, Just Right ("the game") is a puzzle game for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

1. The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are stored on your device.
  • We don't run our own servers. With your consent where the law requires it, the game sends gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see how the game is played and improve it (§4A). They're tied to a random ID for this install, never to your name or email.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described in §4. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first. On iPhone, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so you can keep playing:

  • your level progress and results;
  • your coin and undo-token balances, and a record of how you earned and spent them;
  • your season pass progress and the rewards you've claimed;
  • the decorations and cosmetics you own;
  • your settings, such as sound and whether the move counter is shown.

The game also keeps a simple record of in-game events on your device, for example "level completed" or "hint used". Events that happen before you've answered the consent step stay on your device. They're sent to Google Analytics for Firebase only once allowed (§4A), and deleted if not.

This information stays on your device until you delete the game. Your device's own backup may also include it:

  • on iPhone, an iCloud or computer backup;
  • on Android, Google's Auto Backup.

Those backups are controlled by your device settings and by Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

3. Information handled by others

3.1 Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases: Remove Forced Ads, the No Ads Bundle, a monthly premium season pass, and helper packs (Untangle and Make it fit).

  • Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their terms and privacy policies.
  • We receive only a confirmation that a purchase is valid, so the game can unlock it and restore it on a reinstall.
  • We don't receive your name, payment card or billing address.
  • Our gameplay statistics (§4A) record which product was bought, its price and currency, and the store's transaction number, to measure what players buy. They never include payment details.

3.2 Ads

See §4.

3.3 Cloud save

  • On iPhone: if you're signed in to iCloud, the game can store a copy of your progress record in your iCloud account, so it survives a reinstall and carries over to your other devices. Apple handles this data; we can't access it.
  • On Android: if you're signed in to Google Play Games, the game can store a copy of your progress record in Play Games saved games, so it carries over between devices. Google handles this data; we can't access it.

3.4 Update check

  • On iPhone: the game checks a small file on our website for a newer version. The request carries nothing about you, and we don't log it. The file is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address with the request.
  • On Android: Google Play tells the game when an update is available.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob.

To show ads, prevent fraud and measure how ads perform, Google and its partners may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID on Android, and on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address and the approximate location it implies;
  • information about your device and the game, such as the model, OS version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, for example whether you saw or tapped one;
  • crash and performance data related to ads.

Your choices:

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): before showing ads, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form (UMP). If you don't consent, the game requests non-personalised ads only. The game works the same either way. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings › Privacy › Privacy choices. The row appears where the consent form applies.
  • iPhone tracking: after the tutorial, iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA isn't shared, and ads aren't personalised using it. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some devices.
  • Remove Forced Ads: this purchase turns off the ads that show between levels. Optional reward videos, for hints and undos, stay available only if you choose to watch them.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4A. Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and where players get stuck, the game sends gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service.

What's sent: for example, the first launch, tutorial steps, levels started, cleared or left (with time and move counts in ranges), helpers, hints and undos used, season pass progress, which offers and screens were shown, ads seen, and purchases (product, price, currency, transaction number). Each event is tied to a random ID that Firebase creates for this install of the game. The game never sends your name, email, contacts or anything you type, and it doesn't use the advertising ID or IDFA for these statistics.

Also collected by Google: your IP address, used to estimate your country or region, and basic device information such as model, OS version and language.

Consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, statistics are sent only if you agree in the consent form, and you can change this in Settings › Privacy › Privacy choices. In other regions they're on. If you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information in a US state that offers it, ads-related signals are turned off.

Keeping and using it: Google keeps the statistics for up to 14 months in Google Analytics. We also keep a copy of the raw events in Google BigQuery for longer-term analysis. We use the statistics to improve the game and to measure our own ad campaigns (for example, which campaign led to an install or purchase). We don't sell them.

Remote settings: the game also fetches a few settings from Firebase Remote Config, for example how often ads show between levels. A small share of new players is randomly placed in a group that sees no forced ads, so we can measure their effect fairly.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Firebase privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Children

The game is made for a general audience. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

  • Google Play target audience: 13 and over.
  • If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it. We hold no names or contact details, only the gameplay statistics in §4A, tied to a random install ID.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information.

  • We don't hold your name or contact details. The gameplay statistics in §4A are tied to a random install ID. To have them deleted, contact us with that ID, shown in Settings › Privacy, or use your consent choices to stop new ones being sent.
  • To delete the game's data on your device, delete the game.
  • For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's controls at https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • For any question or request, contact us at contact@ghoroury.com.
  • We don't sell personal information. Where the law calls ad personalisation "sharing", you can opt out through the consent and tracking choices in §4.

7. Security

Your game data is stored using your device's standard app storage. We don't transmit it to our own servers. Gameplay statistics (§4A) travel to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and are stored in our Google Analytics and Google Cloud (BigQuery) projects, with access limited to us.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Where the change is significant, we'll also note it in the game's update notes.
