Step Maze

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · Applies to Step Maze for iPhone

Step Maze does not collect your data. The developer runs no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What the game keeps lives on your iPhone or in your own iCloud account, which the developer cannot read. What it sends goes only to Apple's own services, when you use them.

What the app reads, and where it stays

  • Steps (Motion & Fitness). The game moves your explorer with the steps your iPhone already counts. Your step data is read on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • Apple Health (optional). With your permission, the app reads your recent step counts to suggest a starting difficulty and to show, on the Health tab, how much of your walking happened in a maze. These counts are read on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • Location (optional). Only from the Passport screen, the app checks where you are to see whether you are near a landmark. Each location is used for that moment only. It is never stored and never uploaded. When you stamp a landmark, the app saves only which landmark and when, never where you were.

What the app writes

  • Apple Health (optional). One walking workout for each maze you finish, so your maze walks appear in your workout history. The app never writes step counts or distance.
  • Your game data. Your finished runs, streak, coins, purchases, stamps and settings are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, they sync through your own iCloud account's private database, so your streak survives a new phone. That database is yours; the developer cannot see it. With iCloud signed out, everything stays on the device.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Game Center (Apple), if you are signed in. The app sends your maze scores, a number recording how many hints a daily run used, and your achievement progress. Leaderboards show other players' display names and scores; the app shows them while you look at a board and does not keep them. Your Game Center profile name is shown on the boards you post to. You can change it in Settings → Game Center.
  • The App Store (Apple). Purchases go through Apple's StoreKit. The developer never sees your payment details.
  • Sharing (your choice). When you tap Share on a results screen, the app makes one picture of that run's card and hands it to the iOS share sheet. The card shows the maze, your score or steps, and your streak. It never shows your name, a place or a Health figure. Where it goes next is your choice.

Nothing else leaves your iPhone. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it does not track you across apps or websites. Data sent to Apple's services is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you have enabled Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymized crash reports with the developer. You control this in iOS settings.

Coins and purchases

Coins you buy are not restorable. Apple does not allow restoring consumable purchases, so your coins live in your game data: they come back after a reinstall, or on a new phone, only if you are signed in to the same iCloud account. The theme pack is restorable — use Restore Purchases in the shop.

Children

Step Maze collects no data from anyone, so it collects no data from children.

Your choices

  • You can turn off Motion & Fitness, Apple Health and Location at any time in the iOS Settings app. The game tells you what stops working. Without Motion & Fitness the explorer cannot move; everything else works without Apple Health or Location.
  • You can sign out of Game Center or iCloud in the iOS Settings app.
  • Deleting the app deletes the game data on your iPhone. Data in your own iCloud account is managed from Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
