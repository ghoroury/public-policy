Unciphered — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 August 2026

Unciphered is an offline word game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no social features. This policy describes the little that remains.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Your progress — solved phrases, coins, hints, streak, settings — is stored in a file on your device and nowhere else. We operate no servers and cannot see your data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app keeps a local record of completed transaction identifiers solely so a purchase is never credited twice; that record stays on your device.

Advertising

Unciphered shows one optional rewarded ad: after losing a level, you may choose to watch an ad for an extra try. You are never required to watch one — the same extra try is available for in-game coins, and when no ad can be shown the extra try is given free.

Ads are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). We configure them as non-personalised on every request, restrict them to G-rated creative, and the app never requests App Tracking Transparency permission — so the advertising identifier is not available to the ad SDK and no cross-app tracking occurs.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect device identifiers, coarse location (derived from IP address), advertising interaction data, and diagnostic data. Google describes this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and its Privacy Policy.

Your choices in the EEA and UK

Where the law requires it, a consent form is shown before any ad can be requested. You can change your answer at any time in Settings ▸ Ad privacy inside the app. Declining consent never limits the game: every level remains fully playable, and the extra try is given free when no ad is available.

Children

Unciphered is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13.
