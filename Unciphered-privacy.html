Unciphered — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026, for version 2.0.2 and later on iOS and Android

Unciphered is an offline word game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no social features. The app itself sends nothing anywhere. The one exception is the Unciphered App Clip, the instant one-puzzle version that opens from a link, which reports anonymous play statistics. This policy describes both, and the Android app, which works the same way as the iOS app.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Your progress — solved phrases, coins, hints, streak, settings — is stored in a file on your device and nowhere else. We operate no servers and cannot see your data. Deleting the app deletes it.

The App Clip

The App Clip lets you play a single puzzle from a link without installing the app. To learn which puzzles make a good first game, and which links and ads bring players, it sends one anonymous event at each step of that puzzle: opened, solved, ran out of lives, left without solving, used a hint, or tapped “Get Unciphered”. Each event says:

  • which puzzle it was;
  • where the link came from, when the link says so (for example, which ad campaign);
  • for the end of a puzzle: how long it took, how many hints were used and how many lives were lost;
  • the App Clip’s version number.

No name, account, email, device identifier or advertising identifier is ever sent, and no location. Nothing sent can be connected to you or used to follow you across apps or websites, and we do not track you. Events are sent to our server at unciphered.ghoroury.dev, run on Cloudflare, which stores only the event itself (not your IP address) and deletes it automatically after a limited period. We use it only to improve the game and our ads; it is never sold or shared.

If you solve a puzzle in the App Clip and then install the app, the app picks up that puzzle as solved. This happens on your device, through storage the App Clip and the app share; nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Share links

You can share a puzzle with a link. Opening one of these links in the app sends nothing. If a link is opened in a web browser instead, the page at unciphered.ghoroury.dev counts the visit anonymously, as its own privacy page explains. No cookies are used.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app keeps a local record of completed transaction identifiers solely so a purchase is never credited twice; that record stays on your device.

Advertising

Unciphered contains no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Running out of tries offers an extra try — free, or for in-game coins earned by playing.

We advertise Unciphered elsewhere. When you arrive from one of those ads, the link names the campaign, and the App Clip includes that name in the events described above. That is how we learn which ads work; it involves no identifier and no tracking.

A future version may introduce an optional rewarded ad (watch an ad for an extra try, never required). If that happens, this policy will be updated first, and the app’s App Store privacy label will change with it.

Feedback and ratings

The app may, rarely, ask whether you are enjoying it. Choosing to rate opens Apple’s own App Store review prompt; choosing to send feedback opens a short Google Form in your browser. Both are optional, both leave the app, and the app records only that it asked. Anything you write in the form is handled by Google under its privacy policy and read by us.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the app can send you up to two notifications a day — one around midday when a new puzzle is ready, and one in the evening when that day’s secret level is still within reach. They are scheduled on your device by the app itself: nothing is sent to a server, no device token is created, and we are not told whether a notification was delivered, opened or ignored. Reminders are off until you ask for them, and you can turn them off again in Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Android

Unciphered for Android works the same way. The app keeps your progress in one file on your device and sends nothing anywhere; there is no App Clip on Android. Coin packs and chapter collections are sold through Google Play Billing and processed entirely by Google; we never see your payment details, and the app stores only the transaction identifiers of completed purchases so a purchase is never credited twice. Choosing to rate opens Google Play’s own in-app review prompt. Optional daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device and can be turned off in the app’s Settings or in Android Settings at any time. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDKs other than Google Play’s billing and review libraries.

Children

Unciphered is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13.
