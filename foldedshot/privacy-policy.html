FoldedShot

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Applies to FoldedShot for iPhone

FoldedShot does not collect your data. The game works entirely offline: there are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What the game saves stays on your iPhone.

What the app uses

  • The fold of your iPhone. On iPhone Duo, the game reads whether the phone is open or closed, and how far it is folded, so that folding the phone can fire your shot. This is read on your iPhone, used only while you play, and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Touches on the screen. Where you place targets and aim is used only to play the game on your iPhone.

The app does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Apple Health or any other personal data.

What the app saves

  • The match in progress. The targets, shots and turn of the current match are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone, so you can close the app and resume later.
  • Whether you have seen How to Play. So the guided practice shot opens by itself only the first time.

Nothing else is saved. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What is sent, and to whom

Nothing. FoldedShot does not connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, has no in-app purchases, and does not track you across apps or websites.

If you have enabled Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymized crash reports with the developer. You control this in iOS settings. Apple handles this under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

FoldedShot collects no data from anyone, so it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, for example if a later version adds an in-app purchase, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
