FoldedShot

A two-player duel for one phone. Aim on your side, fold to fire, and open to see where your shot landed across the fold. Made for iPhone Duo, and playable on any iPhone with a simulated fold.

Privacy Policy

Common questions

The app says “Unfold to play”. On iPhone Duo, the game is played on the inner screen. Open the phone fully, or lay it flat.

I folded the phone, but nothing fired. Tap Lock Shot first. Folding without locking never uses up your turn.

How do I fire on an iPhone without a fold? After Lock Shot, tap Fold. A countdown plays; then tap Open to reveal your shot.

Where does my shot land? At the mirror image of your aim point: the same distance from the fold, on your opponent’s side.

I closed the app partway through a match. Open it again and tap Resume Match. The match continues from the same turn.
